Suspected GAPs Creation for Claims (Methods – 1,2, &10)
Overview
Analyze medical and pharmacy claims to find missing Hierarchical Condition Categories (HCCs) that could be documented based on specific clinical indicators defined in GAP methods.
METHOD_IDMETHOD1On RX, no relevant CC2NOS/NEC, no relevant CC4Prior Year CC10High Risk Proc/Dx, no relevant CC
Key Steps
1. Method Metadata & Method Metadata code groups.
* Load method metadata (Methods 1, 2, and 10 for CMS MA) in to reference table
* Filters for inclusion-only rules (where EXCLUSION_TYPE_1 and EXCLUSION_TYPE_2 are "NA")
* These methods define clinical indicators (diagnosis codes, CPT/HCPCS codes, NDCs) that suggest specific HCCs
2. Claims Data Preparation
* Professional Claims: Outpatient/physician claims with diagnoses
* Facility Claims: Hospital/institutional claims with diagnoses
* Pharmacy Claims: Prescription drug claims (NDC codes)
* Pseudo Claims: All MAO-004 based claims with Diagnoses
* Combines all claim types and joins with qualification status where applicable.
3. Draft Gap Identification
Method 1 (Pharmacy Drug based gaps):
* Joins method metadata with pharmacy claims on NDC codes
* Identifies HCCs suggested by specific medications
Methods 2 (NOS/NEC Diagnoses based gaps):
* Joins method metadata with medical claims on:
o Diagnosis codes (ICD) OR
* Identifies HCCs suggested by procedures/diagnoses
Methods 10 (High Risk Procedures based gaps):
* Joins method metadata with medical claims on:
o CPT/HCPCS procedure codes OR
o ICD procedure codes
* Identifies HCCs suggested by procedures/diagnoses
* Exclusion Logic 
Scenario 1 (Multi-step with modifiers):
1. S1: Find claims with specific CPT/HCPCS codes
2. S2: Verify modifier codes exist in the same claim
3. S3: Check if exclusion diagnoses exist in last 180 days
4. Create GAPs: S2 - S3 (claims without exclusions)
Scenario 2 (Direct exclusion check):
1. S1: Find claims with specific CPT/HCPCS codes (no modifier present)
2. S2: Check if exclusion diagnoses exist in last 180 days
3. Create GAPs: S1 - S2 (claims without exclusions)
4. Hierarchy Suppresion on Draft GAPs 
* Suppress lower severity Draft GAPs based on Hierarchy rules. (ref_hierarchy_config)
* Add Y or N based on results of the above rule in Suppression1_YN column.
5. Gap Validation Against Risk Scores
* Identify existing member HCC assignments from risk_member_output or risk_member_hcc
* Compares all draft gaps(Suppression1_YN column=’N’ OR ‘Y’) against existing member HCCs( list of HCC code from risk_member_output).
* Mark gaps as "Y" (already documented) or "N" (true gap) based on match up.
* All draft GAPs with "N" (true gap) will be qualifying to create GAPs at this stage.
6. Hierarchy Suppresion on Final GAPs 
* Apply Hierarchy suppression on all the draft gap (MATCHING_YN column=’N’) with again at this stage (Suppress lower severity Draft GAPs based on Hierarchy rules. (ref_hierarchy_config) and write all unsuppressed final GAPs to ‘Suspected GAPs’ table with ‘Open Suspect’ status. 
7. Member GAPs Summary View
* Provides Final GAPs summary view by member, method, Risk Year, Dx Source and condition category and count of Suspected HCC (based on distinct Claims) – Add all columns that are needed for this Table/View.
* Add Confidence Factor - Based on frequency in the Risk Year, find the confidence factor from ref_method_frequency table.
o Frequency =no. of times that HCC or DIAG codes appeared during the Risk Year on different claims (distinct Claim BIDs).
o Confidence factor = PERCENT_WEIGHT from ref_method_frequency table based on Frequency.
o Note: Confidence factor is currently available for Method 10 only, for method 1 & 2, populate PERCENT_WEIGHT from ref_method_metadata table AS-IS in this column.
Suspected GAPs Creation for Claims (Method 4)
Method 4: Prior Year Chronic Condition Methodology
This methodology identifies members who had a diagnosis for a chronic or persistent condition in previous 2 years but do not have a claim for that condition in the current year. Since chronic conditions should be documented every year, missing diagnoses may indicate a documentation gap or missed coding opportunity. 
Logic:
- Collect all diagnoses for each member over the current and previous two years.
- Focus on chronic/persistent conditions that should be coded every year – See BCBSA document (Medicare_Advantage_Methodologies_Association_NOV2025_working_RAKESH.docx
- Use reference Table (ref_chronic_condition) for Chronic CC list
- Flag members who had chronic condition in prior 2 years but not in the current year.
- Create opportunities for these missing CCs for that member and assign a confidence level based on frequency and recency of prior diagnoses based on ‘ref_method_prior_year’ reference table.
Example:  
- Member A had diabetes diagnoses in 2022 and 2023, but not in 2024. Member A is flagged as an opportunity to create a GAP.
Prior Year Claims Processing
Persistent = Based on prior years (last 2 years). 
* To call an HCC an actual persistent in 2025 RISK Year (current Year) for a member, it should be a chronic HCC and that chronic HCC (or a lower severity HCC) should have been identified for the same member in Risk Year 2023 or 2024 (previous 2 years) and should also be identified in Risk Year 2025 OR if a chronic HCC appears first time in 2025 (higher Severity HCC) with Suspect status as 'Confirmed Non Suspect'. 
* To call an HCC an Open persistent in 2025 RISK Year(current Year) for a member, it should be a chronic HCC and that chronic HCC (or a lower severity HCC) should have been identified for the same member in Risk Year 2023 or 2024 (previous 2 years), but and should NOT be identified in Risk Year 2025. If a chronic HCC appears first time in 2025 (lower Severity HCC) then 'Open NS Persistent' if it is lower severity HCC with Suspect status as 'Open Non Suspect'

Adding Confidence Factor to Persistence based GAPs:
Based on duration & frequency find the confidence factor from ref_method_prior_year table.
Duration = Days difference between first claim date the HCC was identified in prior year period and the date monthly cycle was run. 
Frequency =no. of times that HCC or DIAG codes appeared during the prior year period on different claims (distinct Claim BIDs).
Confidence factor = PERCENT_WEIGHT from ref_method_prior_year table where Duration is between LAST_DX_OCCURANCE_MIN & LAST_DX_OCCURANCE_MAX and Frequency is between DX_FREQUENCY_MIN & DX_FREQUENCY_MAX
 
*Currently in On-prem for ref_method_prior_year table, LAST_DX_OCCURANCE is not captured as MIN or MAX but just as one column, for cloud we will enhance to capture MIN & MAX for better handling.
Final Suspected GAPs (All Methods)
Consolidate suspected GAPs data from above methods (1,2,4, &10) in to single view/Table.
For Open GAP = 
	Member_BID, Risk Year, Method_ID, SAS_Model_Year, SAS_Model_Version, qualified_claim, CC_Code (HCC) where create GAP status is ‘Create GAP’
Business Purpose
This analysis helps healthcare organizations:
* Identify coding opportunities: Find conditions that are clinically indicated but not documented
* Improve risk adjustment: Ensure accurate HCC capture for Medicare Advantage plans
* Support clinical documentation: Flag cases where additional diagnosis coding may be warranted
* Optimize revenue: Proper HCC documentation impacts risk-adjusted payments
