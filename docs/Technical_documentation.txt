Project Overview
CMS Risk Adjustment
CMS Risk Adjustment is a methodology used by the Centers for Medicare & Medicaid Services (CMS) to ensure that health plans are fairly compensated for the risk profile of their enrolled members. It adjusts payments based on the health status and demographic characteristics of beneficiaries.
Why It Matters:
•	Fair Reimbursement:
Plans serving sicker or more complex patients receive higher payments to reflect the expected cost of care.
•	Encourages Accurate Documentation:
Providers and plans are incentivized to document all relevant diagnoses to ensure proper risk scoring.
•	Supports Value-Based Care:
Accurate risk scores help align incentives with patient outcomes and care quality.
How It Works:
•	Use claims and encounter data to identify diagnoses. Use member demographic data to classify the member in right category & model.
•	Diagnoses are then mapped to Hierarchical Condition Categories (HCCs), which are weighed to calculate a Risk Adjustment score (aka RAF).
•	The RAF score influences the capitation payment CMS makes to Medicare Advantage and ACA plans.
GAP Suspecting
Diagnosis GAP Suspecting is a process used to identify missing or undocumented chronic conditions in a member’s medical record that could impact their risk score and care management. These gaps often occur when a condition was previously documented but not captured in the current reporting period.
How It Works:
•	Compares historical diagnoses with current-year claims and encounters.
•	Applying Suspecting Methodology rules to flag potential missing conditions.
•	Generates suspect lists (CHASE) for provider review or outreach teams.
Key Benefits of Risk Adjustment & Dx GAP Suspecting:
•	Revenue Optimization:
Accurate risk scores directly impact plan revenue and capturing all eligible diagnoses helps avoid underpayment from CMS
•	Compliance & Audit Readiness:
Proper documentation reduces audit risk and ensures regulatory compliance.
•	Population Health Insights:
Helps identify high-risk members for care management and intervention.
•	Operational Efficiency:
Prioritized GAP suspect lists streamline chart reviews and provider engagement.

This documentation describes a comprehensive Medicare Advantage (MA) risk adjustment system that calculates CMS Hierarchical Condition Category (HCC) risk scores for Medicare beneficiaries. The system processes member enrollment data, medical claims, and diagnosis codes to determine payment risk scores used for Medicare Advantage plan reimbursement.

Processing Workflow
Step 1: Data Loading and Preparation
load_and_prepare_data(spark, source_schema, config_schema, plan_id,
contract, community_model, version, time_period,
config_dict, year_month, plan_name, hcc_map_column,
model, incl_supplemental_mmr)
Purpose: Loads member enrollment, diagnosis data, and all configuration tables with appropriate filters.
Key Operations:
•	Filters member data by plan_id, contract, and year_month
•	Filters diagnosis data by time_period date ranges
•	Loads configuration tables with model/version filters
•	Validates data availability
Step 2: Data Enrichment
enrich_claims_with_member_data(risk_member, risk_member_diag)
Purpose: Joins member demographic data with diagnosis claims data.
Key Operations:
•	Calculates member age as of February 1st of risk year
•	Joins on memb_id_cd (member identifier)
•	Creates enriched dataset with demographics and diagnoses
Step 3: Disability Status Calculation
get_original_disability(input_risk_score_df, score_config)
Purpose: Determines disability and original enrollment reason status.
Logic:
•	DISABL = 1 if age < 65 AND OREC ≠ "0", else 0
•	ORIGDS = 1 if OREC = "1" AND DISABL = 0, else 0
Where OREC is the Original Reason for Entitlement Code.
Step 4: Community Model Assignment
assign_community_model(get_original_disability_df, community_model_rules)
Purpose: Assigns members to community model segments based on conditional rules.
Key Features:
•	Evaluates rules in priority order (SNPNE_ models first, then NE_ models)
•	Uses SQL expressions for complex conditional logic
•	Assigns segments like "New_Enrollee", "CSNP", etc.
•	Defaults to "Unknown" if no rules match
Step 5: Age/Sex/Disease Interaction (SEDITS) Processing
age_sex_sedits(community_model_df, version, sedits_diag_codes, icd_to_hcc_mapping)
Purpose: Applies age, sex, and diagnosis-specific HCC assignment rules.
Version-Specific Rules:
V28 Rules:
•	Female-specific diagnoses → HCC 112
•	Age < 18 specific diagnoses → Exclude (-1)
•	Age < 50 specific diagnoses → HCC 22
•	Age ≥ 2 specific diagnoses → Exclude (-1)
V24 Rules:
•	Female-specific diagnoses → HCC 48
•	Age < 18 specific diagnoses → HCC 112
•	Age outside 6-18 range specific diagnoses → Exclude (-1)
Step 6: ICD to HCC Mapping
map_icd_to_hcc(age_sex_sedits_df, version, sedits_diag_codes, hcc_map_column)
Purpose: Maps ICD-10 diagnosis codes to HCC categories and aggregates by member.
Key Operations:
•	Joins diagnosis codes with HCC mapping table
•	Groups by member and collects unique HCC codes
•	Creates diagnostic code to HCC mapping audit trail
•	Removes duplicate HCC assignments
Step 7: HCC Hierarchy Application
apply_hcc_hierarchy_rules(icd_hcc_df, hierarchy_config)
Purpose: Applies HCC hierarchy suppression rules where higher-level conditions suppress lower-level ones.
Logic:
•	Identifies parent-child HCC relationships
•	Suppresses child HCCs when parent HCC is present
•	Ensures only the most severe condition category is counted
•	Example: HCC 18 (Diabetes with complications) suppresses HCC 19 (Diabetes without complications)
Step 8: Score Assignment
assign_scores(hcc_hierarchy_df, score_config)
Purpose: Assigns coefficient scores for demographics, HCCs, and special categories.
Score Components:
1. Demographic Score: Based on age, gender, and community model
•	Coefficient pattern: {community_model}_{gender}{age}
•	Example: "cms_hcc_community_M75" for 75-year-old male
2. HCC Score: Sum of all applicable HCC coefficients
•	Coefficient pattern: {community_model}_HCC{hcc_code}
•	Example: "cms_hcc_community_HCC18" for diabetes with complications
3. Original Disability Score: For originally disabled beneficiaries
•	Coefficient pattern: {community_model}_OriginallyDisabled_{gender}
•	Applied when ORIGDS = 1
4. HCC Count Payment Score: Based on total number of HCCs
•	Pattern: {community_model}_D{count} or {community_model}_D10P for 10+
•	Accounts for disease burden complexity
Step 9: Interaction Score Calculation
add_interaction_rules_and_calculate_score(scored_df, interaction_config)
Purpose: Calculates additional scores for HCC combinations that indicate increased risk.
Logic:
•	Evaluates predefined HCC combinations
•	Assigns interaction scores when multiple related conditions exist
•	Example: Diabetes + Chronic Kidney Disease interaction
•	Sums all applicable interaction scores per member
Step 10: Final Risk Score Calculation
calculate_final_scores(interaction_score_df)
Purpose: Combines all score components into final raw risk score.
Formula:
Raw_Risk_Score 	= demog_score + hcc_score + hcc_count_payment_score + 					    interaction_score + origds_score
Step 11: Score Normalization
normalization_scores(final_scores_df, normalization_factors)
Purpose: Applies CMS normalization factors and coding pattern adjustments.
Calculations:
Normalized_Risk_Score = Raw_Risk_Score / normalization_factor
Payment_Risk_Score = Normalized_Risk_Score × (1 - coding_pattern_adjustment)
Step 12: Weighted Risk Score
weighted_risk_score(normalized_scores_df, version_weightage_risk_year)
Purpose: Applies version-specific weighting factors for final payment calculation.
Formula:
Weighted_Risk_Score = Payment_Risk_Score × weightage

3.3.7 HEALTH_EVENTS_MOR Business context:
STORED PROCEDURE: GENERATE_MOR_HEALTH_EVENTS - BUSINESS EXPLANATION
Purpose
Processes Medicare Monthly Operational Report (MOR) files to identify chronic health conditions for risk adjustment and payment calculations.

Business Context
WHAT IS A MOR FILE?
•	Monthly report from Medicare Advantage plans to CMS
•	Contains condition codes (HCC/RXHCC) for each member
•	Stored as a 200-character fixed-width string (FULL_RECORD)
•	Each position in the string = a specific health condition flag (0 or 1)
WHY PROCESS MOR FILES?
1.	Risk Adjustment: CMS pays more for sicker patients
2.	Historical Tracking: Chronic conditions persist over time
3.	Confidence Scoring: More years with condition = higher reliability
4.	Payment Accuracy: Prevents over/under-payment

Business Logic Flow
STEP 1: IDENTIFY PAYMENT YEARS TO PROCESS (LINES 27-42)
-- Get years from new MOR files
SELECT DISTINCT mor.PAYMENT_YEAR
FROM MEDICARE_MOR mor
JOIN adm_FileCounts a ON a.FileSID = mor.FILE_SID
WHERE a.FileType = 'MOR' AND a.RecordsGenerated = 0
 
Business Logic:
•	Only process new files (RecordsGenerated = 0)
•	Add +1 and +2 years to the list for lookback
Example:
New MOR file received for: 2025
Years to process: [2025, 2026, 2027]

Why? Because:
- 2025 records need to check 2023, 2024, 2025 history
- 2026 records need to check 2024, 2025, 2026 history  
- 2027 records need to check 2025, 2026, 2027 history
 

STEP 2: BUILD CHRONIC CONDITIONS REFERENCE (LINES 82-114)
SELECT
    MODEL_VERSION = SUBSTRING(sm.SAS_MODEL_VERSION, 5, 2)
    , cc.CC_ID
    , cc.CC_CODE
    , CHRONIC = MAX(CASE WHEN scc.CHRONIC = 1 THEN 1 ELSE 0 END)
    , ACUTE = MAX(CASE WHEN scc.CHRONIC = 0 THEN 1 ELSE 0 END)
FROM ref_sas_model sm
JOIN ref_risk_hcc cc ...
WHERE sm.PROGRAM = 'CMS'
GROUP BY ...
HAVING MAX(CASE WHEN scc.CHRONIC = 1 THEN 1 ELSE 0 END) = 1
 
Business Logic:
•	Identify which condition codes are chronic (persist over time)
•	Examples: Diabetes, COPD, Cancer = Chronic
•	Examples: Pneumonia, Flu = Acute (not chronic)
Example Output:
MODEL_VERSION | CC_ID | CC_CODE | CHRONIC | ACUTE
24           | 10    | HCC010  | 1       | 0     (Fully chronic: Diabetes)
24           | 186   | HCC186  | 1       | 1     (Partially chronic: Cancer - can resolve)
 

STEP 3: PROCESS EACH PAYMENT YEAR (LOOP LINES 120-241)
For each year, the procedure:
3A. GET MOST RECENT MOR RECORDS (LINES 143-164)
SELECT mor.*
FROM MEDICARE_MOR mor
WHERE PAYMENT_YEAR BETWEEN @PaymentYear - 2 AND @PaymentYear
-- Keep only MOST RECENT per PAYMENT_YEAR + HICN
ROW_NUMBER() OVER (PARTITION BY PAYMENT_YEAR, HICN 
                   ORDER BY PAYMENT_DATE DESC, RUN_DATE DESC)
 
Business Logic:
•	Get 3 years of history (e.g., 2023, 2024, 2025)
•	Keep only latest submission if member has multiple files
•	Only process members with new files (RecordsGenerated = 0)

Member 123456789:

3B. PARSE CHRONIC CONDITIONS (LINES 167-203)
SELECT 
    mor.HICN
    , EVENT_YEAR = @PaymentYear - mor.PAYMENT_YEAR  -- 0=Current, 1=Prior, 2=2nd Prior
    , chronic.CC_CODE
    , HAS_CONDITION = 1
FROM #MOR mor
JOIN ref_MOR_field_position cc ON cc.RECORD_TYPE = mor.RECORD_TYPE
JOIN #ChronicConditions chronic ON chronic.CC_ID = cc.HEALTH_EVENT_CC_ID
WHERE SUBSTRING(mor.FULL_RECORD, cc.FIELD_POSITION, 1) = 1  -- Check position in string
PIVOT (MAX(HAS_CONDITION) FOR EVENT_YEAR IN ([0],[1],[2]))
 
Business Logic:
•	FULL_RECORD = 200-character string with condition flags
•	ref_MOR_field_position = lookup table mapping positions to conditions
•	Check if position = '1' → member has that condition
•	PIVOT: Convert years into columns
Example:
Member 123456789 FULL_RECORD:
Position:  1   2   3  ... 45  46  47  ...
Value:     0   1   0  ... 1   0   1   ...
           ↑   ↑       ↑       ↑

47            | 18    | HCC018  (COPD)
 
Check positions:

Position 47 = '1' → Has COPD (HCC018)
 
Build history:

2024 MOR: Position 45='1', Position 47='1'  → Had Diabetes & COPD
2023 MOR: Position 45='0', Position 47='1'  → No Diabetes, Had COPD
 
After PIVOT:

3.4.1 Pseudo Claims:
Pseudo Claims:
	Pseudo Claims are synthetic claim records generated by the process when a real, matching claim cannot be found for a given member/service/date cluster in the MAO supplemental data.
Why does Pseudo claim?
•	When we process MAO data, we try to match each supplemental record to an existing, qualified claim (facility or professional).
•	If no such claim exists for a given member/service/date, we still need a claim record to attach the supplemental diagnosis codes for risk adjustment or reporting.
•	In this case, we generate a "pseudo" claim, a placeholder claim with a unique ID (often starting with "PSEUDO" and a sequence number). 
How are pseudo claims handled in the process?
•	We create a new claim ID (e.g., 'PSEUDO' + [sequence]).
•	we insert this into the claims tables (PROFESSIONAL_ID, PROFESSIONAL, PROFESSIONAL_CASE, etc.).
•	we link the supplemental diagnosis codes to this pseudo claim so they are included in downstream analytics and reporting.

CASE

  WHEN TRIM(field) RLIKE '^[0-9]{4}-[0-9]{2}-[0-9]{2}$' THEN to_date(TRIM(field), 'yyyy-MM-dd')

END

1.	Unpivot 38 diagnosis code pairs from horizontal to vertical format
2.	Match MAO encounters to existing claims (Facility/Professional)
3.	Generate pseudo claims when no match exists
4.	Apply diagnosis adjustments (Add/Delete/Keep) to supplemental tables
Input: MEDICARE_MAO (loaded by Load_Medicare_MAO.sql)
Output: Supplemental diagnosis tables linked to claims for HCC risk scoring
WorkFlow:
MEDICARE_MAO (38 columns)
    ↓ LATERAL VIEW STACK
medicare_mao_vertical (1 row per diagnosis)
    ↓ ROW_NUMBER() OVER (PARTITION BY MBI, Diag_Code, ...)
medicare_mao_vertical_dedup (DupSeq = 1)
    ↓ WHERE Allowed_Flag NOT IN ('D','N') AND Diag_Flag IN ('D','A','')
new_records
    ↓ MERGE
SUPPLEMENTAL_MAO (generates SUPPLEMENTAL_BID)
    ↓ JOIN
new_supplemental_bids
    ↓ JOIN medicare_mao_vertical_dedup
SUPPLEMENTAL_MAO_DETAIL (one row per diagnosis)
    ↓ JOIN
SUPPLEMENTAL_ID (tracks file type)
    ↓ JOIN MEMBER → JOIN medical_claims
new_mao_claims
    ↓ JOIN FACILITY/PROFESSIONAL claims
mao_claims_matched
    ├─→ MERGE → SUPPLEMENTAL_MAO_CLAIM (matched claims)
    └─→ (NO MATCH) → unmatched_mao_claims
              ↓ Generate PSEUDO claims
              ├─→ MERGE → PROFESSIONAL_ID
              ├─→ MERGE → RISK_PROV
              ├─→ MERGE → PROFESSIONAL
              └─→ MERGE → SUPPLEMENTAL_MAO_CLAIM
                     ↓
matched_claims (all claims with supplemental data)
    ├─→ supplemental_diagnosis (from MAO)
    └─→ existing_diagnosis (from claims)
              ↓ UNION (Add + Keep + Delete logic)
tmp_CHASE_DIAG_ADJUSTMENTS
    ├─→ MERGE → FACILITY_DIAG_SUPPLEMENTAL (Inpatient/Outpatient)
    └─→ MERGE → PROFESSIONAL_DIAG_SUPPLEMENTAL (Professional)

SECTION 1: Data Preparation
Step 1.1: Unpivot Diagnosis Codes
Temp View: medicare_mao_vertical
Purpose: Transform 38 diagnosis code columns (horizontal) into multiple rows (vertical).
Key Logic:
LATERAL VIEW STACK(
  38,
  Diag_Code_01, Diag_Flag_01, Diag_Code_02, Diag_Flag_02, ...
) AS Diag_Code, Diag_Flag
Filters Applied:
•	Diag_Code IS NOT NULL
•	TRIM(Diag_Code) <> '' (exclude empty codes)
ICD Version Handling:
CASE WHEN ICD_Type = 0 THEN 10 ELSE ICD_Type END AS ICD_Type
Rule: If ICD_Type = 0, default to ICD-10.
Output Schema:
Column	Description
Report_ID, Contract_ID	File identifiers
MBI	Medicare Beneficiary Identifier (member)
ICN	Internal Control Number (claim ID)
DOS_From, DOS_Thru	Date of Service range
Service_Type	'I' (Inpatient), 'O' (Outpatient), 'P' (Professional)
Submit_Date	Date submitted to CMS
Allowed_Flag	'A' (Allowed), 'D' (Disallowed), 'N' (Not Allowed)
ICD_Type	ICD version (9 or 10)
Diag_Code	Diagnosis code (e.g., E11.9)
Diag_Flag	Action: 'A' (Add), 'D' (Delete), '' (Keep)
Step 1.2: Deduplication
Temp View: medicare_mao_vertical_dedup
Purpose: Handle duplicate diagnosis codes for the same encounter.
Deduplication Logic:
ROW_NUMBER() OVER (
  PARTITION BY MBI, Diag_Code, DOS_From, DOS_Thru, Service_Type
  ORDER BY Submit_Date DESC
) AS DupSeq
Rule: For duplicate (MBI, Diag_Code, DOS_From, DOS_Thru, Service_Type) combinations, keep the most recent submission (DupSeq = 1).
SECTION 2: Supplemental MAO Processing
Step 2.1: Identify New Records
Temp View: new_records_raw
Filters Applied:
WHERE DupSeq = 1
  AND Allowed_Flag NOT IN ('D', 'N')  -- Only allowed encounters
  AND Diag_Flag IN ('D', 'A', '')     -- Valid diagnosis flags
Business Rule: Only process allowed encounters with valid diagnosis actions.
Output: Distinct (MBI, DOS_From, DOS_Thru, Service_Type) combinations.
________________________________________
Step 2.2: Deduplicate New Records
Temp View: new_records
Purpose: Remove duplicates within the same batch.
ROW_NUMBER() OVER (
  PARTITION BY MBI, DOS_From, DOS_Thru, Service_Type
  ORDER BY LOAD_DATE DESC
) AS rn
WHERE rn = 1
________________________________________
Step 2.3: MERGE into SUPPLEMENTAL_MAO
Target Table: SUPPLEMENTAL_MAO
Purpose: Create cluster-level records for each unique encounter.
Match Condition:
ON target.MBI = source.MBI
   AND target.DOS_FROM = source.DOS_From
   AND target.DOS_THRU = source.DOS_Thru
   AND target.SERVICE_TYPE = source.Service_Type
Actions:
•	MATCHED: Update LOAD_DATE, SOURCE_LOAD_MONTH, audit fields
•	NOT MATCHED: Insert new encounter record
Key Field: SUPPLEMENTAL_BID (auto-generated surrogate key via Delta Lake identity column or external sequence)
________________________________________
Step 2.4: Capture Generated IDs
Temp View: new_supplemental_bids
Purpose: Link newly inserted encounters to their generated SUPPLEMENTAL_BID.
SELECT
  n.MBI, n.DOS_From, n.DOS_Thru, n.Service_Type,
  s.SUPPLEMENTAL_BID  -- ← Generated ID
FROM new_records n
INNER JOIN SUPPLEMENTAL_MAO s ON (natural keys match)
________________________________________
SECTION 3: Supplemental MAO Detail
Step 3.1: Prepare Detail Records
Temp View: supplemental_detail_source
Purpose: Create one row per diagnosis code for each encounter.
Key Fields:
•	DX_RECORD_IDENTIFIER: Dense rank by (ICD_Type, Diag_Code) within encounter
•	DX_RECORD_SEQUENCE: Original DupSeq from deduplication
Deduplication (within batch):
ROW_NUMBER() OVER (
  PARTITION BY SUPPLEMENTAL_BID, DupSeq, ICD_Type, Diag_Code
  ORDER BY Submit_Date DESC
) AS rn
WHERE rn = 1
________________________________________
Step 3.2: MERGE into SUPPLEMENTAL_MAO_DETAIL
Target Table: SUPPLEMENTAL_MAO_DETAIL
Match Condition:
	ON target.SUPPLEMENTAL_BID = source.SUPPLEMENTAL_BID
   AND target.DX_RECORD_IDENTIFIER = source.DX_RECORD_IDENTIFIER
   AND target.DX_RECORD_SEQUENCE = source.DX_RECORD_SEQUENCE
Actions:
•	MATCHED: Update diagnosis code, flags, dates, audit fields
•	NOT MATCHED: Insert new diagnosis detail record

SECTION 5: Claims Matching
Step 5.1: Build MAO Claims View
Temp View: new_mao_claims
Purpose: Enrich MAO encounters with member and provider type data.
Provider Type Mapping:
CASE Service_Type
  WHEN 'I' THEN 'HI'  -- Hospital Inpatient
  WHEN 'O' THEN 'HO'  -- Hospital Outpatient
  WHEN 'P' THEN 'PH'  -- Professional/Physician
END AS MA_PROVIDER_TYPE
Joins:
•	SUPPLEMENTAL_MAO: Get encounter details
•	MEMBER: Lookup MEMBER_BID by MBI (de-identified member ID)
Overview
MAO claims data with member identification information using conditional crosswalk logic based on home plan configuration.
________________________________________
Purpose
Build a unified view of MAO claims that correctly identifies members using either:
•	MEDICARE_MBI_XWALK table for plans requiring MBI crosswalk (e.g., HOME_PLAN_ID_CD = '170')
•	MEMBER table for plans using de-identified member IDs (e.g., HOME_PLAN_ID_CD = '123', '20')

Query Logic Flow
1. Base Join
FROM new_supplemental_bids nsb
INNER JOIN SUPPLEMENTAL_MAO mao
  ON mao.SUPPLEMENTAL_BID = nsb.SUPPLEMENTAL_BID
•	Type: INNER JOIN
•	Purpose: Get only MAO claims for new supplemental bids
•	Cardinality: 1:N (one bid can have multiple claims)
2. Crosswalk Path (Case 1)
LEFT JOIN MEDICARE_MBI_XWALK mmx
  ON mmx.MBI = mao.MBI

LEFT JOIN ref_home_plan rhp_mmx
  ON rhp_mmx.HOME_PLAN_ID_CD = mmx.HOME_PLAN_ID_CD
  AND rhp_mmx.IS_ACTIVE_PLAN = TRUE
  AND rhp_mmx.MBI_XWALK_REQUIRED = TRUE

•	Trigger Condition: MBI_XWALK_REQUIRED = TRUE
•	Join Logic:
1.	Match mao.MBI to mmx.MBI
2.	Validate plan configuration via ref_home_plan
•	Returns: mmx.MEMB_ID_CD, mmx.HOME_PLAN_ID_CD
•	Active For: HOME_PLAN_ID_CD = '170'
3. Member Path (Case 2)
LEFT JOIN (
  SELECT DISTINCT HOME_PLAN_ID_CD, DEID_MEM_ID, MEMBER_BID
  FROM MEMBER
) mem
  ON mem.DEID_MEM_ID = mao.MBI

LEFT JOIN ref_home_plan rhp_mem
  ON rhp_mem.HOME_PLAN_ID_CD = mem.HOME_PLAN_ID_CD
  AND rhp_mem.IS_ACTIVE_PLAN = TRUE
  AND rhp_mem.MBI_XWALK_REQUIRED = FALSE
•	Trigger Condition: MBI_XWALK_REQUIRED = FALSE
•	Join Logic:
1.	Match mao.MBI to mem.DEID_MEM_ID
2.	Validate plan configuration via ref_home_plan
•	Returns: mem.MEMBER_BID, mem.HOME_PLAN_ID_CD
•	Active For: HOME_PLAN_ID_CD IN ('123', '20')
4. Validation Filter
WHERE COALESCE(rhp_mmx.HOME_PLAN_ID_CD, rhp_mem.HOME_PLAN_ID_CD) IS NOT NULL
•	Purpose: Ensure at least one valid join path succeeded
•	Rejects:
o	Claims with invalid MBI
o	Inactive plans
o	Plans with incorrect crosswalk configuration

Step 5.2: Find Matching Qualified Claims
Temp View: mao_claims_matched
Purpose: Match MAO encounters to existing medical claims.
Matching Criteria:
Criterion	Logic
Member	c.MEMBER_BID = p.MEMBER_BID
Provider Type	c.CLAIM_PROVIDER_TYPE = p.MA_PROVIDER_TYPE
Date Overlap	c.FIRST_CLAIM_DATE <= p.DOS_THRU AND c.LAST_CLAIM_DATE >= p.DOS_THRU
Pseudo Exclusion	CLM_ID_CD NOT LIKE 'PSEUDO%'
Program	PROGRAM = 'CMS' (Medicare claims only)
Claim Sources:
1.	Facility Claims (CLM_TP_CD IN (1, 2)):
o	Type 1: Inpatient → Provider Type 'HI'
o	Type 2: Outpatient → Provider Type 'HO'
2.	Professional Claims (CLM_TP_CD = 3):
o	Type 3: Professional → Provider Type 'PH'
Step 5.3: Insert Matched Claims
Target Table: SUPPLEMENTAL_MAO_CLAIM
Purpose: Link SUPPLEMENTAL_BID to matched claim.
Tiebreaker Logic:
ROW_NUMBER() OVER (
  PARTITION BY SUPPLEMENTAL_BID
  ORDER BY LAST_CLAIM_DATE DESC, CLAIM_BID DESC
) AS VISIT_RANK
WHERE VISIT_RANK = 1
Rule: If multiple claims match, use the most recent claim (by LAST_CLAIM_DATE). If tied, use the highest CLAIM_BID.
Match Condition:
ON target.SUPPLEMENTAL_BID = source.SUPPLEMENTAL_BID
   AND target.CLAIM_TYPE = source.CLAIM_TYPE
   AND target.CLAIM_ID = source.CLAIM_ID
Fields:
Column	Description
SUPPLEMENTAL_BID	FK to SUPPLEMENTAL_MAO
CLAIM_TYPE	'1' (Inpatient), '2' (Outpatient), '3' (Professional)
CLAIM_ID	FK to FACILITY or PROFESSIONAL table
QUALIFIED	1 (claim is qualified for risk adjustment)
RISK_BID	MEMBER_BID (member identifier)

Step 7.4: Diagnosis Adjustment Logic
Temp View: tmp_CHASE_DIAG_ADJUSTMENTS
Purpose: Determine which codes to Add, Delete, or Keep.
Three UNION Queries:
________________________________________
Query 1: ADDED Codes
SELECT
  s.CLAIM_TYPE, s.CLAIM_ID, s.DIAG_CD, s.ICD_VER,
  'A' AS SUPPLEMENTAL_CODE  --  Mark as Added
FROM supplemental_diagnosis s
LEFT ANTI JOIN existing_diagnosis e ON (match keys)
WHERE s.SUPPLEMENTAL_CODE <> 'D'
Logic:
•	Codes in supplemental_diagnosis (MAO file)
•	NOT in existing_diagnosis (current claim)
•	NOT marked for deletion (SUPPLEMENTAL_CODE <> 'D')
Result: New diagnosis codes to add.
________________________________________
Query 2: KEPT Codes
SELECT
  e.CLAIM_TYPE, e.CLAIM_ID, e.DIAG_CD, e.ICD_VER,
  '' AS SUPPLEMENTAL_CODE  --  Mark as Kept (empty string)
FROM existing_diagnosis e
LEFT ANTI JOIN (
  SELECT * FROM supplemental_diagnosis WHERE SUPPLEMENTAL_CODE = 'D'
) s ON (match keys)
Logic:
•	Codes in existing_diagnosis (current claim)
•	NOT in supplemental_diagnosis with SUPPLEMENTAL_CODE = 'D' (not marked for deletion)
Result: Existing codes to preserve.
Business Rule (Line 620 Comment in SQL Server version):
"Per the Business Owners, Existing Diagnosis Codes that were not in the MAO file should be kept and not deleted."
________________________________________
Query 3: DELETED Codes
SELECT
  CLAIM_TYPE, CLAIM_ID, DIAG_CD, ICD_VER,
  'D' AS SUPPLEMENTAL_CODE  --  Mark as Deleted
FROM supplemental_diagnosis
WHERE SUPPLEMENTAL_CODE = 'D'
Logic:
•	Codes in supplemental_diagnosis with SUPPLEMENTAL_CODE = 'D'
Result: Codes explicitly marked for deletion in MAO file.
________________________________________

ON target.FACIL_HDR_BID = source.FACIL_HDR_BID

   AND target.ICD_VER = source.ICD_VER
   AND target.SUPPLEMENTAL_CODE = source.SUPPLEMENTAL_CODE
   AND target.CHASE_PROJECT_SUPPLEMENTAL_BID = source.CHASE_PROJECT_SUPPLEMENTAL_BID
Actions:
•	MATCHED: Update SUPPLEMENTAL_DATE, FILE_TYPE, LOAD_DATE, audit fields
•	NOT MATCHED: Insert new diagnosis adjustment record
Filter: WHERE tmp.CLAIM_TYPE IN ('1','2') (Facility claims only)
Schema:
Column	Description
FACIL_HDR_BID	FK to FACILITY claim
DIAG_CD	Diagnosis code
ICD_VER	ICD version
SUPPLEMENTAL_CODE	'A' (Add), 'D' (Delete), '' (Keep)
SUPPLEMENTAL_DATE	CMS submission date
FILE_TYPE	'Mao_Supp_Dx' (or parameter value)
CHASE_PROJECT_SUPPLEMENTAL_BID	Links to SUPPLEMENTAL_ID

Objective:
 Automate and enforce data quality checks on ingested tables using Great Expectations (GE), with YAML-driven rules and Spark integration. This module validates all data loaded into Delta Lake tables against configurable expectations, ensuring compliance and reliability.

Integration Points
•	Pipeline Entry:
 src\spark\data_loader\data_loader_main.py
•	Validation Logic:
 src\spark\helpers\ge_util.py
•	Rule Definitions:
 config\great_expectations\dq_validation_rules.yml

Processing Workflow
1.	Load Data Quality Rules
a.	Function: get_validation_checks(checks_dir, validation_step_name)
 Path: src\spark\helpers\ge_util.py
 Loads table-specific validation rules from YAML files.
2.	Initialize Great Expectations Context
a.	Function: get_ge_context(validation_step_name, connection_string="")
 Path: src\spark\helpers\ge_util.py
 Sets up a GE context with a Spark datasource for the current pipeline stage.
3.	Execute Validation Suite
a.	Function: get_validation_results(validation_meta_data, df, context, stage_name)
 Path: src\spark\helpers\ge_util.py
 Iterates over tables and applies all configured expectations to the Spark DataFrame.
 Internally calls:
i.	perform_validation(context, validation_step_name, data, meta, name) (same file): Runs all expectations for a table.
ii.	run_single_expectation(context, stage_name, sub_data, expectation_meta, name) (same file): Executes individual expectations.
iii.	flatten_ge_result_fields(res) (same file): Flattens and parses GE result objects.
4.	Save and Log Results
a.	Function: save_results(validation_results, ge_stage_name, spark)
 Path: src\spark\helpers\ge_util.py
 Converts validation results to a Spark DataFrame, formats, previews, and logs them.
 (Optionally writes to a summary table for audit and reporting.)
 
Configuration Structure
•	Rule Definitions:
 YAML-based, per table, under config\great_expectations\dq_validation_rules.yml
•	Schema Mapping:
 Uses expected_schema.yaml for field validation.
•	Severity Levels:
 error, warning, info (used for pipeline pass/fail logic).
•	Execution:
 Invoked from data_loader_main.py after data ingestion and before transformation.
 
Example Rule (YAML Snippet)
 
Expectation Types Reference
Below is a summary of each expectation_type used in the YAML, with its purpose and validation logic:
Expectation Type	Purpose/Validation Logic
expect_table_row_count_to_be_between	Ensures the table has a row count within specified min/max bounds.
expect_column_values_to_not_be_null	Checks that all values in the specified column are not null.
expect_column_values_to_be_of_type	Validates that all values in the column match the expected data type (e.g., IntegerType, NumericType).
expect_column_values_to_be_between	Ensures column values fall within a specified numeric range (min/max).
expect_column_values_to_match_regex	Validates that column values match a given regular expression pattern (e.g., date format, code format).
expect_column_values_to_be_in_set	Checks that column values are members of a specified set of allowed values.
expect_column_values_to_be_unique	Ensures all values in the column are unique (no duplicates).
expect_column_value_lengths_to_be_between	Validates that string column values have lengths within specified min/max bounds.
 
Output and Reporting
·	Validation results are logged and previewed in the pipeline.
·	Structured logs can be written to Delta tables for audit and compliance.
·	Severity-based failover: If any rule with severity error fails, ingestion for that table is halted.

1. join_facility_and_detail()
Purpose: Joins facility header, detail, and member enrollment data.
Function Signature:
def join_facility_and_detail(
     facility_df: DataFrame,
     facility_detail_df: DataFrame,
     member_enrollment: DataFrame
 ) -> DataFrame
Business Logic:
1.	Left join `facility_detail_df` with `facility_df` on `FACILITY_BID`
2.	Inner join with `member_enrollment` on `MEMBER_BID` to ensure valid membership
3.	Drop duplicate `FACILITY_BID` column from detail table
Input Tables:
Table	Key Columns	Purpose
-------	-------------	---------
facility_df	FACILITY_BID, MEMBER_BID	Claim header information
facility_detail_df	FACILITY_BID, CLM_LN_NUM	Claim line-level details
member_enrollment	MEMBER_BID, COVRG_BEGIN_DT, COVRG_END_DT	Member coverage dates
Example:
Facility Header:

2. filter_outpatient_claims()
Purpose: Identifies qualifying outpatient claims based on CMS criteria.
Function Signature:
def filter_outpatient_claims(df: DataFrame) -> DataFrame
**Qualification Criteria (ALL must be true):**
Criterion	Condition	Reason
-----------	-----------	--------
Allowed Amount	`ALLWD_AMT > 0`	Must have financial value
Claim Type	`CLM_TP_CD == 2`	Outpatient facility claim
Population	`POP_ID == "05"`	Medicare Advantage population
Payment Status	`CLM_PMT_STS_CD == "P"`	Paid claims only
Service Dates	`FIRST_SERV_DT >= COVRG_BEGIN_DT AND LAST_SERV_DT <= COVRG_END_DT`	Within coverage period
Bill Type	`TP_OF_BILL_CD starts with "13" OR "85"`	Outpatient hospital or critical access hospital
Output:
·	Adds column `QUALIFY_CLAIMS`: "Y" if all criteria met, "N" otherwise
Example:
Input:
 +--------+----------+------+---------------+----------------+--------------+
 |ALLWD_AMT|CLM_TP_CD|POP_ID|CLM_PMT_STS_CD |TP_OF_BILL_CD   |FIRST_SERV_DT |
 +--------+----------+------+---------------+----------------+--------------+
 | 1500.00| 2        | 05   | P             | 131            | 2024-01-15   |
 | 0.00   | 2        | 05   | P             | 131            | 2024-01-16   |
 | 2000.00| 2        | 01   | P             | 131            | 2024-01-17   |
 +--------+----------+------+---------------+----------------+--------------+
 
Output (with QUALIFY_CLAIMS column):
 +--------+----------+------+---------------+----------------+--------------+---------------+
 |ALLWD_AMT|CLM_TP_CD|POP_ID|CLM_PMT_STS_CD |TP_OF_BILL_CD   |FIRST_SERV_DT |QUALIFY_CLAIMS |
 +--------+----------+------+---------------+----------------+--------------+---------------+
 | 1500.00| 2        | 05   | P             | 131            | 2024-01-15   | Y             |
 | 0.00   | 2        | 05   | P             | 131            | 2024-01-16   | N             | (ALLWD_AMT = 0)
 | 2000.00| 2        | 01   | P             | 131            | 2024-01-17   | N             | (POP_ID != 05)
 +--------+----------+------+---------------+----------------+--------------+---------------+

3. filter_inpatient_claims()
Purpose:Identifies qualifying inpatient claims based on CMS criteria.
Function Signature:
def filter_inpatient_claims(df: DataFrame) -> DataFrame
Qualification Criteria (ALL must be true):
Criterion	Condition	Reason
-----------	-----------	--------
Claim Type	`CLM_TP_CD == 1`	Inpatient facility claim
Population	`POP_ID == "05"`	Medicare Advantage population
Payment Status	`CLM_PMT_STS_CD == "P"`	Paid claims only
Service Dates	`FIRST_SERV_DT >= COVRG_BEGIN_DT AND LAST_SERV_DT <= COVRG_END_DT`	Within coverage period
Bill Type	`TP_OF_BILL_CD starts with "11" OR "41"`	Inpatient hospital or religious non-medical
Output:
·	Adds column `QUALIFY_CLAIMS`: "Y" if all criteria met, "N" otherwise
·	Adds column `INVALID_CPT_HCPCS`: Set to "" (empty) for all inpatient claims
Note: Inpatient claims don't require CPT/HCPCS validation, unlike outpatient claims.
4. process_facility_claims()
Purpose: Processes both outpatient and inpatient claims, validates procedure codes, and applies window functions for group-level qualification.
Function Signature:
def process_facility_claims(
     outpatient_claims: DataFrame,
     risk_proc_qualify: DataFrame,
     inpatient_claims: DataFrame
 ) -> DataFrame
Processing Steps:
Step 1: Separate Qualified vs Unqualified Outpatient Claims
outpatient_qualified = outpatient_claims.filter(col("QUALIFY_CLAIMS") == "Y")
 outpatient_unqualified = outpatient_claims.filter(col("QUALIFY_CLAIMS") == "N")
Step 2: Validate CPT/HCPCS Codes for Qualified Claims
Join Condition:
JOIN risk_proc_qualify ON:
   - claim.CPT_AND_HCPCS_CD = qual.CPT_AND_HCPCS_CD
   - claim.STMT_THRU_DT BETWEEN qual.EFFECTIVE_DATE AND qual.EXPIRATION_DATE
Example:
Qualified Outpatient Claim:
 +-------------+----------------+--------------+
 |FACILITY_BID |CPT_AND_HCPCS_CD|STMT_THRU_DT  |
 +-------------+----------------+--------------+
 | FAC001      | 99213          | 2024-01-15   |
 | FAC001      | 99999          | 2024-01-15   |  (Invalid CPT)
 +-------------+----------------+--------------+
 
Risk Proc Qualify Table:
 +----------------+--------------+--------------+--------------------+
 |CPT_AND_HCPCS_CD|EFFECTIVE_DATE|EXPIRATION_DATE|CMS_ELIGIBILITY_FLAG|
 +----------------+--------------+--------------+--------------------+
 | 99213          | 2024-01-01   | 2024-12-31   | 1                  |
 +----------------+--------------+--------------+--------------------+
 
After Join:
 +-------------+----------------+--------------------+
 |FACILITY_BID |CPT_AND_HCPCS_CD|CMS_ELIGIBILITY_FLAG|
 +-------------+----------------+--------------------+
 | FAC001      | 99213          | 1                  |  (Match found)
 | FAC001      | 99999          | NULL               |  (No match)
 +-------------+----------------+--------------------+
Step 3: Apply Window Function for Group-Level Qualification
Business Rule: If ANY line item in a `FACILITY_BID` group has a matching CPT code, ALL lines in that group qualify.
CREATE FLAG: 1 IF MATCHED, 0 IF NOT
PROPAGATE MATCH ACROSS ENTIRE GROUP
window_spec = Window.partitionBy("FACILITY_BID")
 
has_match = WHEN(CMS_ELIGIBILITY_FLAG IS NOT NULL, 1).OTHERWISE(0)
 
QUALIFY_CLAIMS = WHEN(MAX(has_match) OVER window_spec > 0, "Y").OTHERWISE("N")
Example:
Before Window Function:
 +-------------+-----------+----------------+--------------------+---------+
 |FACILITY_BID |CLM_LN_NUM |CPT_AND_HCPCS_CD|CMS_ELIGIBILITY_FLAG|has_match|
 +-------------+-----------+----------------+--------------------+---------+
 | FAC001      | 1         | 99213          | 1                  | 1       |
 | FAC001      | 2         | 99999          | NULL               | 0       |
 | FAC001      | 3         | 88888          | NULL               | 0       |
 +-------------+-----------+----------------+--------------------+---------+
 
After Window Function (MAX(has_match) = 1 for FAC001):
 +-------------+-----------+----------------+---------------+
 |FACILITY_BID |CLM_LN_NUM |CPT_AND_HCPCS_CD|QUALIFY_CLAIMS |
 +-------------+-----------+----------------+---------------+
 | FAC001      | 1         | 99213          | Y             |  (Matched)
 | FAC001      | 2         | 99999          | Y             |  (Group qualified)
 | FAC001      | 3         | 88888          | Y             |  (Group qualified)
 +-------------+-----------+----------------+---------------+
Step 4: Set INVALID_CPT_HCPCS Flag
Logic:
·	`INVALID_CPT_HCPCS = "Y"` if `CMS_ELIGIBILITY_FLAG IS NULL` (no match found)
·	`INVALID_CPT_HCPCS = ""` if match found
+-------------+-----------+----------------+--------------------+------------------+
 |FACILITY_BID |CLM_LN_NUM |CPT_AND_HCPCS_CD|CMS_ELIGIBILITY_FLAG|INVALID_CPT_HCPCS |
 +-------------+-----------+----------------+--------------------+------------------+
 | FAC001      | 1         | 99213          | 1                  | ""               |
 | FAC001      | 2         | 99999          | NULL               | Y                |
 +-------------+-----------+----------------+--------------------+------------------+
 Step 5: Union All Claims
final_facility_claims = inpatient_claims
     .unionByName(outpatient_qualified_joined)
     .unionByName(outpatient_unqualified)

5. process_profother_claims()
Purpose: Processes professional (provider) claims with member enrollment validation and procedure code validation.
Function Signature:
def process_profother_claims(
     profother: DataFrame,
     member_enrollment: DataFrame,
     risk_proc_qualify: DataFrame
 ) -> DataFrame
Processing Steps:
Step 1: Join with Member Enrollment
professional_df = profother.join(
     member_enrollment.select("MEMBER_BID", "COVRG_BEGIN_DT", "COVRG_END_DT"),
     on="MEMBER_BID",
     how="inner"  # Only keep claims with valid membership
 )
Step 2: Apply Qualification Criteria
Criterion	Condition
-----------	-----------
Payment Status	`CLM_PMT_STS_CD == "P"`
Population	`POP_ID == "05"`
Service Dates	`FIRST_SERV_DT >= COVRG_BEGIN_DT AND LAST_SERV_DT <= COVRG_END_DT`
·	Adds `QUALIFY_CLAIMS` column: "Y" or "N"
·	Adds `CLM_TP_CD` column: Set to "3" (professional claim type)
Step 3: Validate CPT Codes (similar to facility claims)
Join Condition:
JOIN risk_proc_qualify ON:
   - pfo.CPT_AND_HCPCS_CD = qual.CPT_AND_HCPCS_CD
   - pfo.LAST_SERV_DT BETWEEN qual.EFFECTIVE_DATE AND qual.EXPIRATION_DATE
Step 4: Apply Window Function
Window: Partitioned by `PROFESSIONAL_BID`
Logic: Same as facility claims - if ANY line in a `PROFESSIONAL_BID` group matches, ALL lines qualify.
window_spec = Window.partitionBy("PROFESSIONAL_BID")
 QUALIFY_CLAIMS = WHEN(MAX(has_match) OVER window_spec > 0, "Y").OTHERWISE("N")
Step 5: Set Flags and Union
·	`INVALID_CPT_HCPCS = "Y"` if no match, "" otherwise
·	Union qualified and unqualified claims

DEDUPLICATION
Deduplication Logic
Purpose: Remove duplicate claim lines based on business key and keep the most recently updated record.
DEFINE WINDOW: PARTITION BY BUSINESS KEY, ORDER BY MOST RECENT UPDATE
ADD ROW NUMBER AND KEEP ONLY ROW 1 (MOST RECENT)
window_spec = Window.partitionBy("CLAIM_BID", "CLM_LN_NUM", "CLM_TP_CD") \
                .orderBy(
                    coalesce(
                        col("UPDATED_DATE"),
                        col("CREATED_DATE"),
                        current_timestamp()
                    ).desc()
                )
 
deduped_medical_claims_df = medical_claims_df \
     .withColumn("row_num", row_number().over(window_spec)) \
     .filter(col("row_num") == 1) \
     .drop("row_num")
Business Key:
·	`CLAIM_BID`: Unique claim identifier
·	`CLM_LN_NUM`: Claim line number
·	`CLM_TP_CD`: Claim type code (1=Inpatient, 2=Outpatient, 3=Professional)
Ordering Logic:
8.	Use `UPDATED_DATE` if available (record was modified)
9.	Fallback to `CREATED_DATE` if `UPDATED_DATE` is null (new record)
10.	Fallback to `current_timestamp()` if both are null (safety measure)
11.	Order descending (most recent first)
Example:
Before Deduplication:
 +----------+-----------+-----------+-------------------+-------------------+---------+
 |CLAIM_BID |CLM_LN_NUM |CLM_TP_CD  |CREATED_DATE       |UPDATED_DATE       |row_num  |
 +----------+-----------+-----------+-------------------+-------------------+---------+

PSEUDO CLAIMS PROCESSING SYSTEM - DETAILED DOCUMENTATION
Executive Summary
The Pseudo Claims Processing System is a specialized data pipeline designed to handle **synthetic test claims** (pseudo claims) separately from actual member claims. Pseudo claims are system-generated records used for testing, validation, and quality assurance purposes. This system identifies, processes, and loads these test claims into the medical claims repository with appropriate classification and tracking.
KEY CHARACTERISTICS
Characteristic	Description
----------------	-------------
**Identification**	Claim ID starts with "pseudo" (case-insensitive)
**Claim Type Code**	Always set to `9` (pseudo/test claim indicator)
**Qualification Status**	Automatically marked as "Y" (qualified)
**Processing Priority**	Processed separately from real claims
**Data Isolation**	Clearly tagged for identification and reporting exclusion
BUSINESS VALUE
1. **Risk-Free Testing**: Test system changes without impacting production data
2. **Data Validation**: Verify data quality rules and transformations
3. **Audit Compliance**: Maintain clear separation between test and production data
4. **Training Environment**: Provide realistic data for staff training
5. **System Monitoring**: Use pseudo claims to monitor pipeline health

1. INTRODUCTION
This document outlines the logic and configuration used in the MA Model Input Processing pipeline. The process involves reading member, enrollment, and supplemental data, transforming it according to business rules, and writing the results to curated tables.
2. CONFIGURATION AND SETUP
The pipeline initializes widgets for environment, plan name, month, and year. It loads configuration files from YAML to determine catalog, schema, and column mappings.
Widgets:
·	- env: Environment selection (DEV, QA, UAT, PROD)
·	- plan_name: Plan-specific logic execution
·	- month/year: Time period for data processing
3. DATA SOURCES
·	The following tables are read from the silver schema:
·	- member
·	- member_enrollment
·	- mmr (supplemental)
·	- medical_claims
4. TRANSFORMATION LOGIC
The transformation logic is implemented in the function create_risk_member_df. It performs the following steps:
·	- Joins member and enrollment data
·	- Left joins supplemental MMR data
·	- Applies business rules to derive CSNP and institutional statuses
·	- Generates RISK_MEMBER_ID using MD5 hash
·	- Adds audit columns and deduplicates records
5. OUTPUT TABLES
·	The transformed data is written to the following gold schema tables:
·	- risk_member
·	- risk_member_diag
6. ERROR HANDLING
All major steps are wrapped in try-except blocks. Errors are logged using the logger utility and raised with descriptive messages. 
RISK MEMBER CURATION SYSTEM - CLIENT DOCUMENTATION
Executive Summary
The Risk Member Curation System transforms healthcare claims and member data from the transformation layer (Silver) into analysis-ready datasets in the curation layer (Gold). This system prepares data for risk adjustment calculations, which determine payments to healthcare plans based on member health status and expected healthcare costs.
---
Business Purpose
WHAT IS RISK ADJUSTMENT?
Risk adjustment is a process that adjusts payments to healthcare plans based on the health status of enrolled members. Plans with sicker members receive higher payments to cover expected higher costs.
WHY THIS SYSTEM MATTERS
Business Need	Solution
---------------	----------
**Accurate Payments**	Calculate risk scores based on member diagnoses
**Historical Analysis**	Process data for current and past 3 years
**Compliance**	Meet CMS and HHS regulatory requirements
**Plan-Specific Processing**	Handle multiple healthcare plans independently
**Data Quality**	Ensure clean, deduplicated data for analytics
KEY OUTPUTS
1. **`risk_member`** table - Member demographics, enrollment, and supplemental data
2. **`risk_member_diag`** table - Member diagnoses from qualified claims

Data Quality & Deduplication
DEDUPLICATION STRATEGY
**Purpose:** Ensure data integrity by removing duplicate records while preserving the most recent data.
**Common Pattern:**
CODE BLOCK:
window_spec = Window.partitionBy(business_key_columns) \
.orderBy(coalesce(col("UPDATED_DATE"), col("CREATED_DATE"), current_timestamp()).desc())
deduped_df = df \
.withColumn("row_num", row_number().over(window_spec)) \
.filter(col("row_num") == 1) \
.drop("row_num")
CODE BLOCK:
**Key Concepts:**
1. **Partition By**: Define what makes a record "the same"
2. **Order By**: Choose which version to keep (most recent)
3. **Row Number**: Rank records within each group
4. **Filter row_num = 1**: Keep only the top-ranked record
---
UPSERT STRATEGY
**Purpose:** Insert new records or update existing records in target tables.
**Common Pattern:**
CODE BLOCK:
upsert_delta_update_columns(
spark,
source_df,
"target_table_name",
"schema_name",
upsert_condition
)
CODE BLOCK:
**How It Works:**
1. **Match Check**: Compare source and target using business key
2. **If Match**: UPDATE existing record with new values
3. **If No Match**: INSERT as new record
**Benefits:**
- Idempotent processing (can rerun safely)
- Handles incremental updates
- Maintains audit trail

HCC PERSISTENCE PROCESSING DOCUMENTATION
Overview
This system processes Hierarchical Condition Category (HCC) persistence data for healthcare risk adjustment. It identifies, tracks, and categorizes chronic conditions for members across multiple years, determining which conditions persist, which are new, and which are suspect.

Files
1. MEMBER_PERSISTENT_HCC.PY
Purpose: Helper functions module containing all transformation and join logic for HCC persistence processing.
2. CMS_PERSISTENCE_HCC.PY
Purpose: Main orchestration notebook that executes the HCC persistence workflow using the helper functions.

Key Concepts
STATUS TYPES
•	Persistent Status: 
o	Actual Persistent - Condition confirmed across years
o	Open Persistent - Condition continuing from prior year
o	Open NS Persistent - Open Non-Suspect persistent condition
•	Suspect Status:
o	Open Suspect - Condition needs verification
o	Closed Suspect - Condition verified/resolved
o	Confirmed Non Suspect - Condition confirmed as valid
o	Open Non Suspect - Valid condition being tracked
PROCESSING LOGIC
•	Source (src): Current year HCC data from risk_member_hcc table
•	Target (tgt): Historical persistence data from member_persistent_cc table
•	Prior Years: Configurable lookback period (default: 2 years)

Main Workflow (cms_persistence_hcc)
STEP 1: CONFIGURATION & DATA LOADING
•	Input Parameters:
o	env: Environment (DEV/QA/STG/PROD)
o	plan_name: Healthcare plan identifier
o	month: Processing month (01-12)
o	risk_year: Risk model year
o	prior_years: Number of previous years to consider
•	Data Sources:
o	Source: risk_member_hcc (current HCC diagnoses)
o	Target: member_persistent_cc (historical persistence records)
o	Reference: ref_method_prior_year (confidence factor weights)
o	Config: hierarchy_config (parent-child HCC relationships)
STEP 2: THREE-WAY DATA COMPARISON
Compares current source data against target + prior years to identify:
2A. SOURCE NOT IN TARGET (NEW HCCS)
•	Function: build_src_not_in_tgt()
•	Logic: Records in current year not found in historical data
•	Status: Set to NA (will be processed later for hierarchy)
•	Output: Staged to source_records table
2B. TARGET NOT IN SOURCE (DROPPED HCCS)
•	Function: build_tgt_not_in_src()
•	Logic: Historical records not present in current year
•	Processing:
o	Calculates DURATION (days since last claim)
o	Calculates FREQUNCY (distinct claim count)
o	Assigns CONFIDENCE_FACTOR based on duration/frequency ranges
o	Updates status based on prior year context:
	If already persistent → remains Open Persistent
	If new HCC from prior year → Actual Persistent
	Suspect status transitions to Open Suspect
2C. COMMON IN BOTH (CONTINUING HCCS)
•	Function: build_common_both_sides()
•	Logic: Records present in both current and historical data
•	Two Processing Branches:
Branch A - Open Suspects Confirmed:
o	Function: transform_open_suspects_to_persistent()
o	Condition: SUSPECT_STATUS = 'Open Suspect'
o	Action: 
	Status → Actual Persistent
	Suspect → Closed Suspect
	Clears duration/frequency/confidence
Branch B - New HCCs Confirmed:
o	Function: transform_new_hcc_to_persistent()
o	Condition: PERSISTENT_STATUS_REASON = 'New HCC'
o	Action:
	Status → Actual Persistent
	Suspect → Confirmed Non Suspect
	Clears duration/frequency/confidence
STEP 3: UNION & MERGE TO TARGET
•	Combines all three branches (2a, 2b, 2c)
•	Removes duplicates on business keys
•	Appends to member_persistent_cc table
STEP 4: SOFT DELETE PROCESSING
•	Function: merge_update_target_table()
•	Logic: Sets ACTIVE_IND = 'N' for records with status:
o	Closed Suspect 
o	Open Non Suspect
•	Updates Both Tables:
o	member_persistent_cc (persistence table)
o	risk_member_hcc (source HCC table)
STEP 5: HIERARCHY PROCESSING
Handles parent-child HCC relationships to prevent double-counting conditions.
5A. IDENTIFY OPEN SUSPECTS
•	Function: build_heirarcy_open_suspect()
•	Logic: Joins new source records with open suspects from target
•	Extracts trailing HCC code integers for matching
5B. APPLY HIERARCHY RULES
•	Function: build_hierarchy_persistent_final()
•	Sub-processes:
5b.1 - Join with Hierarchy Config
o	Function: build_heirarchy_open_suspect_join()
o	Identifies parent-child relationships
o	Creates flags:
	SRC_PRIMARY: Source HCC is parent of suspect HCC
	TGT_PRIMARY: Suspect HCC is parent of source HCC
5b.2 - New HCC (No Conflict)
o	Function: transform_hierarchy_open_suspect_new_hcc()
o	Condition: TGT_PRIMARY IS NULL (no hierarchy conflict)
o	Status: 
	Actual Persistent
	Confirmed Non Suspect
	Reason: New HCC
5b.3 - Source is Parent
o	Function: transform_hierarchy_open_suspect_src_primary()
o	Condition: SRC_PRIMARY = True
o	Status:
	Open Persistent
	Open Suspect
o	Meaning: Current HCC is more severe/primary than historical
5b.4 - Target is Parent
o	Function: transform_hierarchy_tgt_primary_open_ns()
o	Condition: TGT_PRIMARY = True
o	Status:
	Open NS Persistent
	Open Non Suspect
o	Meaning: Historical HCC is more severe; current is subordinate
5C. UNION & FINAL WRITE
•	Combines all three hierarchy branches
•	Removes duplicates
•	Appends to member_persistent_cc table
STEP 6: CLEANUP
•	Drops temporary source_records table
•	Logs execution time

Key Helper Functions
JOIN OPERATIONS
•	join_keys_src_to_tgt(): Business key join conditions (src → tgt)
•	anti_join_src_not_in_tgt(): Left anti-join (records only in source)
•	anti_join_tgt_not_in_src(): Left anti-join (records only in target)
•	inner_join_common(): Inner join (common records)
CONFIDENCE FACTOR CALCULATION
•	prior_year_rnk_conf_fac(): 
o	Calculates DURATION (days since last diagnosis)
o	Calculates FREQUNCY (distinct claims per HCC)
o	Assigns CONFIDENCE_FACTOR from lookup table based on:
	Duration ranges (e.g., 1-30, 31-60, 61-90 days)
	Frequency ranges (e.g., 1-2, 3-5, 6+ occurrences)
MERGE OPERATIONS
•	merge_update_set(): Generic MERGE operation wrapper
•	merge_update_target_table(): Soft-deletes records (sets ACTIVE_IND='N')

Business Rules Summary
PERSISTENCE LOGIC
1.	New HCC in Current Year: Marked as Actual Persistent if no prior history
2.	HCC Continues from Prior Year: 
a.	Open Suspect → Closed Suspect (confirmed)
b.	New HCC → Actual Persistent (validated)
3.	HCC Drops in Current Year: Marked Open Persistent or Open Suspect (needs follow-up)
HIERARCHY RULES
•	Parent-Child Relationship: Prevents double-counting related conditions
•	Primary HCC: Takes precedence over subordinate HCCs
•	Conflict Resolution:
o	Current year primary → Open Persistent
o	Prior year primary → Open NS Persistent
o	No conflict → Actual Persistent
STATUS TRANSITIONS
Open Suspect + Confirmed → Closed Suspect + Actual Persistent
New HCC + Confirmed → Confirmed Non Suspect + Actual Persistent
Open Persistent + No longer present → Remains Open (tracked for recurrence)
Closed Suspect / Open Non Suspect → ACTIVE_IND = 'N' (soft delete)
